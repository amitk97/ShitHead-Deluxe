Shed Head Deluxe: Card Game · Privacy Policy · Terms of Use

Delete it in the game

  1. Open Shed Head Deluxe and sign in.
  2. Tap Profile in the header.
  3. Scroll to Danger Zone and tap Delete account, then confirm.

Your public profile, leaderboard entries and push notifications stop straight away. We keep the rest for 7 days in case you change your mind: sign back in during that time and choose Keep my account to cancel. After 7 days your account is erased, including your profile, stats, purchases, Diamonds and items, friends, friend requests, reports, leaderboard entries, username and sign-in. Diamonds and items cannot be recovered afterwards.

Can't get into the game?

Email support.shitheaddeluxe@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my account". We will start the same 7-day deletion and reply to confirm.

Get a copy of your data first

In the game: Profile → Your Data → Download my data.

What is not deleted automatically

Records of finished Ranked and online room games can contain the names shown in that game and are not erased automatically. Email us to have the ones linked to your account removed. See the Privacy Policy for details.
