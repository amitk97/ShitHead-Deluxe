## Full description (max 4000)

Shed Head Deluxe is the classic card game where you race to get rid of every card you hold. The last player left holding cards is the Shed Head.

Everyone is dealt three Face-Down cards, three Face-Up cards and three in their Hand. Swap your best cards into your Face-Up row, play equal or higher than the Pile, burn it with a 10 or four of a kind, and use the special powers of the 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, Jack and Joker to trap your opponents. Pick up the Pile and you are back in the fight.

PLAY YOUR WAY
- Play Computer: four difficulties, from Easy to Boss, that unlock as you win. Works offline.
- The Gauntlet: beat a run of bots with limited lives. Easy, Hard and Boss runs unlock as you level up.
- Play Friends: host or join a room with a code, share an invite link, and use emotes. House Rules let you change the card powers.
- Ranked: one-on-one matches with an Elo rating and tiers from Novice to Master. The server shuffles and checks every move.
- Best of 3, 5 or 7 series between friends.
- 2 vs 2 team games, Cold Blooded mode with harsher rules, and Multiple Decks games for up to 8 players.
- A new Weekly Puzzle every week, with 52 hand-made card puzzles.

PROGRESS AND REWARDS
- Levels 1 to 99 with Diamonds and unlockable avatars, card backs and effects.
- A free Season Pass with 50 tiers every two months.
- Daily and weekly challenges, a daily login streak and seasonal events.
- Daily Lucky Games using free Diamonds: Daily Spin, Scratch Card, Three Cards and Drop Zone.

MAKE IT YOURS
- Shop and Custom: avatars, card backs, tables, decks, frames, emote packs and animated burn, Joker and victory effects, all bought with Diamonds you earn by playing.
- Share your look on your profile and gift items to friends.

LEARN FAST
- A one-minute Quick Start and in-depth lessons for every card, plus a searchable Guide.
- Hold any card to see what it does and whether you can play it.

Free to play with no ads. Nothing in the game is bought with real money. For ages 13 and over. Playing online lets you add friends, chat with preset emotes and report other players.
