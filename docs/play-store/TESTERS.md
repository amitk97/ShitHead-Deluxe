# Closed test kit (12 testers, 14 days)

Google's rule for a new personal developer account: at least 12 testers opted in for 14 days in a row before you can apply for production. Recruit about 20. Check Play Console → Testing → Closed testing for the current wording.

## What testers need
- An Android phone and a Google account (the test link works on Android only).
- To join with your link, install from Google Play, and stay opted in for the full 14 days.
- Activity: no daily play is required, but ask for a few games in the first days and one more visit mid-way. Google asks how engaged testers were when you apply.

## Do not
- Pay or reward people for installs or reviews (against Google's rules). Thanking them is fine.
- Ask for 5-star ratings.

## Where to post (free)
- r/AndroidAppTesters (test swaps are welcome)
- Closed-test swap groups on Reddit, Discord and Facebook ("closed testing 12 testers")
- r/playmygame and r/IndieGaming (say it is a free classic card game and a test)
- Shithead / Shed Head card game groups
- Your own friends, family, work and university contacts

## Post (paste and edit)
> **Testers wanted: Shed Head Deluxe, a free classic card game (Android)**
> I'm about to release Shed Head Deluxe: Card Game on Google Play and Google needs 12 people to test it for 14 days. It's the classic "get rid of all your cards" game: play bots, friends in private rooms, Ranked, daily puzzles and more. No ads, no payments.
> To help: send me the Google account email you use on Play (or join this Google Group: <GROUP LINK>), then open <TEST LINK> on your Android phone and install it. Please keep it installed for 14 days and play a few games. Tell me if anything breaks.
> Happy to test your app in return.

## What to try (put on the test page or send)
1. Play Computer: one game on Easy, then try the Tutorial.
2. Open More Modes: try a Puzzle and Cold Blooded.
3. Sign in with Google and open the Daily Spin.
4. Settings: change something, then close and reopen the app.
5. Note anything confusing, slow or broken, and your phone model.

## Weekly reminder (send in week 1 and week 2)
> Thanks for testing Shed Head Deluxe! Please open it this week and play a game or two. Keep it installed until <END DATE>. Anything broken or confusing? Reply here.

## Tracking
Keep a list of tester emails, the date each joined, and any feedback with your fix. You will use it to answer "how did you test and what did you fix" when applying for production.
