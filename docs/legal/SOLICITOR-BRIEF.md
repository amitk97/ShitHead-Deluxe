# Brief for a solicitor (Shed Head Deluxe: Card Game)

Prepared 25 Nov 2026 so one short instruction covers everything. Ask for a fixed fee.

## The product
Free card game (web app, going to Google Play as a wrapper). Google or email sign-in, usernames, friends, online rooms, Ranked, free "Lucky Games" (daily spin, scratch card, drop game: no real money, no cash value), virtual Diamonds earned by playing, no ads, no payments yet. Owner is in London. Players must be 13 or over (stated, not enforced by an age check).

## 1. Documents to review
- `privacy-policy.html` (UK GDPR / Data Protection Act 2018, Google Play data safety consistency)
- `terms.html` (England and Wales law, Diamonds have no cash value, fair play, AI-made art and sound note)
- `delete-account.html` (Play Store account-deletion page)
- Questions: Is the 13+ position enough without an age gate? Is the deletion wording accurate (server purge in `functions/account.js`; finished online room records are kept and not erased automatically)? Do the Lucky Games count as gambling under UK law (free, no stake, no cash-out)? Is an ICO registration or fee needed?

## 2. Names
- **SHED HEAD** (game name). A US registration exists for clothing (Class 25, Reg. 6185384, Makoff Family Trust). Nothing found yet in UK/EU. Owner will not sell clothing or merchandise under the name. Ask: clearance for games and software (Classes 9, 28, 41) in the UK, EU and US, and whether to file.
- **Cold Blooded** (a game mode name). Found: COLDBLOODED live for MorphMarket, LLC (reptile marketplace), COLDBLOODED pending in Class 41 (Serial 99747396, filed 6 Apr 2026), COLD BLOODED & BIZARRE registered (Serial 88720245). UK and EU not searched. Ask: is the mode name safe, or should it change?
- Earlier renames, for the record: "No Mercy" (an UNO product name) became "No Chill", then "Cold Blooded" after a NO CHILL registration (Class 28) was found. "Champagne Pop" became "Bubbly Pop".

## 3. Artwork and sound
- Much art and sound is AI-generated (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude) or from Pixabay; some is made in Blender. See `docs/ASSET-PROVENANCE.md`. Ask: ownership and licence risk, and what records to keep. Three premium avatars (blue lion, red devil, red bird) are generic heraldic animals that could be mistaken for football club crests; the owner keeps them and keeps them out of store graphics.
