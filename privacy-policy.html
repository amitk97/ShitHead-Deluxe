Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

This page explains what information ShitHead Deluxe collects when you use it, why, and where it's stored. ShitHead Deluxe is a small, independently run project — this policy is written in plain language rather than dense legal text, so you can actually tell what happens to your information.

Information we collect

  • Account information. If you sign in with Google, we receive the name and email address on your Google account. If you create an account with email and password instead, we store the email address you provide.
  • Username. The username you choose is public — other players can see it, including via Friends and match history.
  • Gameplay data. Your Ranked rating, win/loss record, and difficulty progress against bots.
  • Social data. Your friends list, pending friend requests, and any players you've chosen to ignore.
  • Presence. Whether you're currently online, shown to your friends only.
  • Technical error reports. If something crashes, a basic error report (what failed and where in the code) is logged automatically to help fix bugs. These reports aren't linked to your identity.

We don't collect payment information, location data, or contacts, and we don't use advertising or analytics trackers.

How we use this information

  • To sign you in and keep your progress attached to your account across devices.
  • To match you with opponents in Ranked play and let you play with friends via Online Room.
  • To show your username, rating, and online status to people you're friends with.
  • To diagnose and fix bugs when something goes wrong.

Where your information is stored

ShitHead Deluxe is built on Firebase, Google's app development platform. Your account and game data are stored in a Firebase Realtime Database hosted in the EU (europe-west1). Firebase's own privacy practices are governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Sharing

We don't sell your information, and we don't share it with third parties other than Google/Firebase, who host the infrastructure this game runs on.

Your choices

  • You can sign out at any time from Settings.
  • You can remove friends, decline requests, or ignore other players at any time from the Friends menu.
  • To request deletion of your account and associated data, email us using the address below. This isn't yet a self-service option in the app itself.

Children

ShitHead Deluxe is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone in that age group.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to support.shitheaddeluxe@gmail.com.

This policy was written to plainly and accurately describe what the app actually does, for a small independently-run project — it hasn't been reviewed by a lawyer. If ShitHead Deluxe grows to handle payments, ads, or data from a specific regulated region (e.g. children's data under COPPA, or EU users under GDPR in a formal sense), this page should be revisited with proper legal advice at that point.
